📘 User Guide

1. Introduction

Ye website aapko live crypto prices aur trading charts dekhne me help karti hai.

2. Signup Process

  • Homepage open kare
  • Sign Up button pe click kare
  • Apna naam enter kare
  • Signup complete hone ke baad Trade button dikhega

3. Live Price Dashboard

  • Coin ka price real-time update hota hai
  • Green = price increase
  • Red = price decrease
  • High / Low / Volume bhi dikhaya jata hai

4. Trading (Charts)

  • Trade button pe click kare
  • Charts page open hoga
  • Yaha aap price movement analyze kar sakte ho

5. Account

  • Profile icon pe click kare
  • Apni details dekhe
  • Logout option use kare
