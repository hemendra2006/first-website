📊 Trading Rules

1. Risk Management

Ek trade me apna 1-2% capital se zyada risk mat lo.

2. Stop Loss zaroor lagao

Har trade me stop loss hona chahiye. Without SL trading = gambling.

3. Overtrading mat karo

Har movement pe trade mat lo. Quality > Quantity.

4. Emotions control karo

Fear aur greed sabse bada loss karwate hain.

5. Proper Analysis karo

Chart samjho, blindly trade mat karo.

6. Loss accept karo

Har trade profit nahi hota. Loss bhi game ka part hai.

⚠️ Important Warning

Trading me risk hota hai. Sirf utna hi paisa use kare jo aap lose kar sakte ho.
